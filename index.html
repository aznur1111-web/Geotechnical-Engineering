Reference & field guidance

The studio groups common geotechnical relationships and screening methods. Use the governing project code and the actual investigation data for design decisions.

Bangladesh - BNBC 2020
Part 6 includes soils and foundations and is the primary local building-code reference to check for Bangladesh projects.
Eurocode 7
EN 1997 covers geotechnical design and ground investigation as Parts 1 and 2.
USACE - Geotechnical
Useful engineering manuals cover bearing capacity, settlement, investigations and foundation practice.
Common standards
ASTM/ISO test standards can govern SPT, CPT, Atterberg limits, Proctor, permeability, consolidation and shear testing.
